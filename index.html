About

I'm a Robotics Build Engineer at Foundation, a humanoid robotics company in San Francisco. I work on the hardware end to end: designing fixtures, stands, and brackets in SolidWorks, building and dyno-testing planetary gear actuators, and bringing new humanoid robots up from subassembly to a full working system.

Before Foundation, I earned my MS in Robotics from UC Riverside. My work there spanned both sides of robotics: mechanical design and FEA for legged robots, autonomous vehicles, and underwater propulsion, and software work in ROS2 navigation, SLAM, perception, and uncertainty-aware imitation learning deployed on physical robots.

What I care about most is hardware that holds up on the robot, not just in CAD or simulation. I like owning a problem from the first sketch to the last failure fix: designing the part, building it, finding where it breaks, and making it better.
