Legged locomotion / UC Riverside, 2025

12-DOF quadruped

Designed, printed, wired, and programmed a four-legged robot from scratch. I sized 12 servos from joint torque requirements, added a hip adduction mechanism for side-to-side balance, and used FEA to strip material from low-stress regions of the legs.

Getting it to walk meant fixing problems I hadn't planned for: the Arduino reset from voltage sag, servos overheated inside the chassis, and cables fatigued at the joints. I upgraded the power wiring, redesigned the chassis for airflow, and rerouted the harness.

How the system works
  1. A policy learns to navigate from expert demonstrations using DAgger.
  2. Monte Carlo Dropout estimates how unsure the policy is at every step.
  3. When uncertainty rises, the robot slows down, and LiDAR safety zones act as a backstop.
  4. Hard cases feed back into training, so the policy improves where it was weakest.
