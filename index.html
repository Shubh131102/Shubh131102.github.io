About

I'm a Robotics Engineer with an MS from the University of California, Riverside. What sets me apart is that I work across both sides of robotics — hardware and software — with equal depth in each.

On the mechanical side, I design and validate hardware for autonomous systems — sensor mounting systems, structural components, and fabricated assemblies using SolidWorks, ANSYS FEA, GD&T, and DFA. My work spans autonomous vehicles, legged robots, and bio-inspired underwater propulsion.

On the software side, I build full autonomous navigation stacks in ROS2 — SLAM-based mapping, CNN-based perception pipelines, and uncertainty-aware imitation learning validated across multiple environments with zero collision performance.

I understand how mechanical decisions affect software performance and vice versa. That systems-level thinking is what I bring to every project.
